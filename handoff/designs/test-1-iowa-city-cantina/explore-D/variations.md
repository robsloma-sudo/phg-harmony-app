# explore-D: proposed ingredient variations (NOT for the guest menu)

Every spec below is **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval**. None of them appears in menu.html, menu-guest.html or the previews. The quantities are starting points for the bar to test, not house specs. No brands, ABVs or prices are proposed.

## Manhattan (on list: 2 oz rye whiskey, 1 oz sweet vermouth, [bitters hidden], cocktail cherry; coupe, stirred)
1. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Añejo Manhattan": 2 oz añejo tequila, 1 oz sweet vermouth, cocktail cherry; coupe, stirred. It uses the agave specimen already on the register.
2. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Brandy Manhattan": 2 oz cognac VSOP, 1 oz sweet vermouth, cocktail cherry; coupe, stirred.
3. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Perfect Manhattan": 2 oz rye whiskey, 0.5 oz sweet vermouth, 0.5 oz dry vermouth, lemon twist; coupe, stirred. It needs a dry vermouth and lemons, which are not in the venue data.

## Margarita (on list: 2 oz tequila blanco, 0.75 oz fresh lime juice, 0.75 oz orange liqueur, 0.5 oz agave syrup; lime wheel; rocks, shaken)
1. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Tommy's-style": 2 oz tequila blanco, 1 oz fresh lime juice, 0.5 oz agave syrup, no orange liqueur; lime wheel; rocks, shaken.
2. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Añejo Margarita": 2 oz añejo tequila, 0.75 oz fresh lime juice, 0.75 oz orange liqueur, 0.25 oz agave syrup; lime wheel; rocks, shaken.
3. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** Salted rim, half the glass (the salt is not in the venue data).

## House Daiquiri (on list: 2 oz white rum, 0.75 oz fresh lime juice, 0.75 oz house 1:1 demerara syrup; lime coin; coupe, shaken)
1. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Agave Daiquiri": 2 oz white rum, 0.75 oz fresh lime juice, 0.5 oz agave syrup; lime coin; coupe, shaken.
2. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Split base": 1 oz white rum, 1 oz tequila blanco, 0.75 oz fresh lime juice, 0.75 oz demerara syrup; lime coin; coupe, shaken.
3. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** A drier spec: 2 oz white rum, 1 oz fresh lime juice, 0.5 oz demerara syrup.

## Brown Butter Old Fashioned (on list: brown butter-washed bourbon, demerara syrup, aromatic bitters; orange peel, large cube; rocks, stirred; quantities not public)
1. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Brown Butter Añejo Old Fashioned": brown butter-washed añejo tequila in place of bourbon, with agave syrup in place of demerara; orange peel; large cube.
2. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** "Rye Old Fashioned": rye whiskey, demerara syrup, aromatic bitters; orange peel; large cube (no fat-wash, so it uses less prep).
3. **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval.** Swap the garnish to a lemon peel (lemons are not in the venue data).
