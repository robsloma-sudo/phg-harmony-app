## Concept

Lotería de la Cantina, a real tabla of four cards. Top: El Cantarito (44, cocktails, Margarita first) and La Botella (8, agave & brandy); below: La Rosa (41, wine) under El Cantarito and El Barril (9, beer & cider) under La Botella. ## What did not fully land (honest)

- Per-column break, and the lower name bands are not aligned across the gutter (La Rosa's at 570.0 pt, El Barril's at 492.0 pt). With shared rows La Botella would carry 90 pt of empty space.
- To make the per-column break work, La Rosa sits under El Cantarito and El Barril under La Botella (left-right swapped in the lower row from the brief's 'El Barril + La Rosa' listing). With El Barril under El Cantarito, column 1 would be 660 pt against 540 pt and would not fit the page.
- The columns do not end on the same line: column 1 ends at 756.0 pt and column 2 at 720.0 pt, so there is empty cream under El Barril. That space is outside the cards; the 12 pt cap inside the cards was kept rather than padding the cards.
- Face share: the 120 pt face is 44% of La Rosa's 264.0 pt card, above the ~40% target.
