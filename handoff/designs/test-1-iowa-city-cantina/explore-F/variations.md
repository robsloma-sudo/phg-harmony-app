# Explore-F cocktail variations (NOT for the guest menu)

Every spec below is **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval**. None of them appears in menu.html or the
previews. No prices are proposed. Base specs come from ../build/draft_doc.json components.

## Margarita (base: 2 oz tequila blanco · 3/4 oz fresh lime juice · 3/4 oz orange liqueur · 1/2 oz agave syrup; shaken · rocks · lime wheel)
1. **Moonlit Margarita** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Swap 1/2 oz tequila blanco for 1/2 oz mezcal for a smoky "night field" note; salt half-rim.
2. **Lantern Margarita** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Replace agave syrup with 1/2 oz honey syrup; garnish a charred orange wheel.
3. **Blue Hour** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Add 1/4 oz butterfly-pea tea to the base; colour turns violet when lime meets it.

## Manhattan (base: 2 oz rye whiskey · 1 oz sweet vermouth · 2 dashes aromatic bitters [hidden on menu] · cocktail cherry; stirred · coupe)
1. **Adobe Manhattan** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Split base 1 1/2 oz rye + 1/2 oz añejo tequila.
2. **Midnight Manhattan** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Replace 1/2 oz sweet vermouth with 1/2 oz amaro.
3. **Firefly Manhattan** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Add a bar spoon of orange liqueur; expressed orange peel.

## House Daiquiri (base: 2 oz white rum · 3/4 oz fresh lime juice · 3/4 oz house demerara syrup; shaken · coupe · lime coin)
1. **Night Garden Daiquiri** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Muddle 3 mint leaves; fine strain.
2. **Agave Daiquiri** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Swap demerara syrup for 1/2 oz agave syrup and 1/2 oz rum for tequila blanco.
3. **Moon Coin** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Add 1/2 oz coconut water; dehydrated lime coin.

## Brown Butter Old Fashioned (base: 2 oz brown butter-washed bourbon · 1/4 oz demerara syrup · 2 dashes aromatic bitters; stirred · rocks · large cube · orange peel)
1. **Lantern Old Fashioned** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Replace demerara syrup with 1/4 oz agave syrup; add 1 dash orange bitters.
2. **Smoke Over the Field** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Split base 1 1/2 oz washed bourbon + 1/2 oz mezcal.
3. **Adobe Old Fashioned** — PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Add 1 dash mole bitters; cinnamon-stick garnish (dairy note from the butter wash still needs confirming).
