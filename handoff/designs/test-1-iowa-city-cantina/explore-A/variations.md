# Explore A: proposed ingredient variations (NOT for the guest menu)

These are proposals only. None of them appear on menu.html or on the previews. Each one uses only spec-style changes (ratio, glass, method or garnish) that suit the spec-sheet concept. No new brands, ABVs or prices are proposed.

## Margarita (draft: 2 oz tequila blanco / ¾ oz fresh lime juice / ¾ oz orange liqueur / ½ oz agave syrup; rocks, lime wheel, shaken)
1. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Tommy's-style: 2 oz tequila blanco / 1 oz fresh lime juice / ½ oz agave syrup, with no orange liqueur. Rocks, lime wheel, shaken.
2. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Served up: the same draft spec in a coupe, shaken and fine-strained, with a lime wheel.
3. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Añejo swap: 2 oz añejo tequila (already on the back bar list) in place of blanco, rest unchanged.

## Manhattan (draft: 2 oz rye whiskey / 1 oz sweet vermouth / 2 dash aromatic bitters (hidden) / cocktail cherry; coupe, stirred)
1. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Perfect-style split: 2 oz rye / ½ oz sweet vermouth / ½ oz dry vermouth (dry vermouth is not in venue data yet), 2 dash bitters, cherry.
2. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Brandy Manhattan: 2 oz Cognac VSOP (on the list) / 1 oz sweet vermouth / 2 dash aromatic bitters, cherry, stirred, coupe.
3. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Rocks service: the same spec on a large cube in a rocks glass.

## House Daiquiri (draft: 2 oz white rum / ¾ oz fresh lime juice / ¾ oz house demerara syrup; coupe, lime coin, shaken)
1. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Drier ratio: 2 oz white rum / 1 oz fresh lime juice / ¾ oz house demerara syrup.
2. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Agave twist: 2 oz blanco tequila / ¾ oz fresh lime juice / ¾ oz house demerara syrup, coupe, lime coin.
3. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Agave-sweetened: swap the demerara syrup for ½ oz agave syrup (already a Margarita prep).

## Brown Butter Old Fashioned (draft: brown butter-washed bourbon / demerara syrup / aromatic bitters; rocks, large cube, orange peel, stirred; house_recipe hidden)
1. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Agave Old Fashioned: 2 oz añejo tequila / ¼ oz agave syrup / 2 dash aromatic bitters, orange peel, large cube.
2. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Rye base: 2 oz rye whiskey (Manhattan base) / ¼ oz demerara syrup / 2 dash aromatic bitters, orange peel.
3. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Served up: the house spec stirred and served in a coupe with no cube, orange peel.
