## Reading the result (design lead)

- **No new direction beat the control on combined score.** K1 71.7, R21 72.3 and K3 70.9 fall within the measured ±5 panel noise, so they are statistically tied. K2 is clearly behind at 66.8.
- **K1 has the best design-critic score of any recent round (72.5).** It also has the best accuracy score of the four (71.5). The critics credit its concept as ownable ("Iowa row crops meet Jalisco hileras"). They mark it down for execution: the agave bands read as serape trim, and the field sits above the list instead of carrying it.
- **Most of the new directions' lost points and gate fails come from the proposed content, not the concepts.** The causes:
  - Placeholder mezcal prices printed as if final.
  - Mezcal ages inferred.
  - A pour size shown without a "proposed" flag.
  - Internal Iowa-account data on a guest menu.
  - Unflagged ABV and region gaps.
  - "Smoky" flavor claims (K3).

  These are fixable in the manifest. Round 21 carries none of that load because it prints only the 14 approved items.
- **All four share execution faults:**
  - Ingredient names split across lines.
  - Beer and wine lines that are just category names ("Dry red wine").
  - No ingredient line on the zero-proof drinks.
  - Brut Rosé with no glass/bottle label.
  - "11.50" as the only price with cents.
- **Recommendation: take K1 Hileras forward.** It has the highest critic and accuracy scores and the strongest concept. Next build:
  1. Plant the drinks on the contour furrows themselves, so the field *is* the reading area.
  2. Replace the rosette bands with true plan-view agave rows at varied scale.
  3. Use one modular grid across both pages.
  4. Fix the content manifest (TBC marks, mezcal ages off-scale, pour ring, gap flags).

  Keep K3's flavor map in reserve as a secondary insert. Drop K2.
