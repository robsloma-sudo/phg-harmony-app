# TEST-1 round 18: "Where Agave Country Meets the Iowa Prairie at Dusk", Cantina & Cocktail Bar, Iowa City (proposal v18)

## Creative thesis

**Agave country meets the Iowa prairie at dusk. One hand-torn paper horizon holds Iowa corn rows and Jalisco agave rows in
the same perspective, under one setting sun. The two fields meet at a torn seam, and the menu then descends through torn
paper strata from dusk light to night.**

- **Concept words (6):** two horizons, one sun · torn seam · corn rows / agave rows · cut paper · dusk to night · warm hospitality
- **Avoid-list:** the round-17 Bistro architecture (vertical wordmark, art rail, side-rail taglines); lotería and papel picado;
  sombreros, cacti clip-art, skulls, piñatas and serape stripes; neon "fiesta" colour; lettering inside the art; trademarked
  worlds; generic grunge; gold anywhere but the sun; boxed modules; a second illustration style; taglines that state item facts.

**Image-substitution test:** a generic Mexican bar swaps in an agave. This page cannot be swapped, because the Iowa corn-row
field and the agave field share one vanishing point under the sun and meet at a torn seam. Only a cantina in Iowa owns that picture.

**Device → thesis trace**

| Device | Traces to |
|---|---|
| Corn-row field (left) and agave-row field (right), both converging on one vanishing point under the sun | two horizons, one sun |
| Torn diagonal seam with a cream fibre rim where the two fields meet | torn seam |
| Two torn prairie-hill bands (long, gentle Iowa swells) | the Iowa horizon at dusk |
| One agave rosette of two-tone, scissor-faceted leaves, each with a fibre rim and a paper shadow | agave country; cut paper at actual size |
| Gold-leaf sun, setting into the hills, sitting behind the first A of CANTINA and showing through its counter | one sun; the wordmark is part of the collage |
| CANTINA set as cut deep-red paper: scissor-roughened edge, cream fibre rim, paper-on-paper shadow, tracking -0.012em | cut paper; one mark |
| Torn page seam, then three torn paper strata (Cocktails: dusk cream, Spirits: late sand, Beer/Cider/Wine: evening tan) | dusk to night; the section structure is the landscape |
| Night ground band closing the page (and the phone scroll), rising as a prairie swell under the shorter Wine column | night; the empty field has a job |
| Paper-fibre grain: 42 % in the hero, 9 % on the menu strata | cut paper; lower texture contrast near text |
| One italic tagline "¡salud!" (Cocktails head) | warm hospitality, in the venue's language |
| Spanish subheads Clásicos · De la Casa · De Barril · Agave | venue fit (only these four; the rest stay in the draft's English) |
| Dotted price leaders in the price red | functional: item-to-price association |

One illustration grammar: flat cut-paper planes with scissor-faceted or torn edges, a cream fibre rim on every torn edge, one
light from the top right (shadows fall down and to the left, strata lift up), no outlines, no gradients inside shapes except the
sky wash and the sun's leaf light. One image treatment: a warm, low-saturation earth palette, the same fibre grain everywhere
and edges that are never clean vector cuts.

  - The menu starts at 262.69 pt and the left column ends at 756 pt (the bottom margin).
