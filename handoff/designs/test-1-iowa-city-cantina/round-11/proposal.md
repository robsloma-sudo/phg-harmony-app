## Concept

Lotería de la Cantina, a real tabla of four cards (2 + 2): El Cantarito (44) is the featured card, top-left and tallest, over La Botella (8); El Barril (9) over La Rosa (41). Every card has a 3 pt frame in its colour, its traditional Don Clemente number in the top-left corner (marigold numeral on an ink chip), one face composition (cut-paper figure left, cantor verse right, scaled to the card), and the lotería name in the band under the figure (one title style, one size). The drinks list is the card's reverse.
