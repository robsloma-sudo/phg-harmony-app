# explore-B cocktail variations (internal, NOT for the guest menu)

Every spec below is **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval**. None of them appears in menu.html.
They are ideas that fit the courtyard concept (colour planes and calm light). They keep the draft's base structure and use only
generic ingredient types (no brands, ABVs or pour sizes are claimed).

## Margarita (draft: tequila blanco 2 oz, fresh lime 0.75 oz, orange liqueur 0.75 oz, agave syrup 0.5 oz; rocks, lime wheel, shaken)
1. **Rosa wall margarita**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Swap the agave syrup for a hibiscus-agave syrup, which turns the drink rosa mexicano.
2. **Marigold margarita**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Split the lime with fresh orange juice and garnish with an orange wheel.
3. **Still water margarita**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Served up in a coupe, with no garnish.

## Manhattan (draft: rye whiskey 2 oz, sweet vermouth 1 oz, aromatic bitters 2 dash (hidden on menu), cocktail cherry; coupe, stirred)
1. **Añejo Manhattan**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Uses the bar's añejo tequila in place of rye.
2. **Shadow Manhattan**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Half the sweet vermouth becomes an amaro.
3. **Perfect Manhattan**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Split sweet and dry vermouth, with a lemon peel.

## Brown Butter Old Fashioned (draft: brown butter-washed bourbon 2 oz, demerara syrup 0.25 oz, aromatic bitters 2 dash; rocks, large cube, orange peel, stirred)
1. **Brown butter añejo old fashioned**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Brown butter-washed añejo tequila with agave syrup in place of demerara.
2. **Cognac old fashioned**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Half the bourbon becomes the bar's VSOP cognac.
3. **Mole bitters old fashioned**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Mole bitters replace the aromatic bitters.

## House Daiquiri (draft: white rum 2 oz, fresh lime 0.75 oz, house 1:1 demerara syrup 0.75 oz; coupe, lime coin, shaken, fine strain)
1. **Cobalt daiquiri**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Butterfly-pea tea syrup that turns from blue to violet with the lime.
2. **Marigold daiquiri**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Adds a small measure of passion fruit syrup.
3. **Hemingway-style daiquiri**: PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Adds grapefruit and maraschino liqueur and cuts back the syrup.
