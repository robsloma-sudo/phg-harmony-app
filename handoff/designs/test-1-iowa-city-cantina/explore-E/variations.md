# Explore-E: proposed ingredient variations (NOT for the guest menu)

Every line below is **PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval**. None of these appear in menu.html.
They have no prices, brands or ABVs. They only rework ingredients already in the draft, or name generic bar staples.

## Margarita (current: 2 oz tequila blanco + ¾ oz fresh lime + ¾ oz orange liqueur + ½ oz agave syrup; shaken, rocks, lime wheel)
1. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. "Tommy's-style": tequila blanco + fresh lime + agave syrup, no orange liqueur; shaken, rocks, lime wheel.
2. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Salt-rim option: same spec with a half salt rim on the rocks glass.
3. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Up version: same spec, shaken and served in a coupe with a lime coin.

## Manhattan (current: 2 oz rye whiskey + 1 oz sweet vermouth + aromatic bitters [hidden]; stirred, coupe, cocktail cherry)

2. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. "Perfect" Manhattan: split the vermouth ½ oz sweet + ½ oz dry; lemon twist.
3. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. On the rocks: same spec served in a rocks glass over a large cube.

## House Daiquiri (current: 2 oz white rum + ¾ oz fresh lime + ¾ oz house demerara syrup; shaken, coupe, lime coin)

3. PROPOSED — not on the venue's list, needs Rob/venue approval. Agave daiquiri (a bridge to the Margarita board): agave syrup instead of demerara.
